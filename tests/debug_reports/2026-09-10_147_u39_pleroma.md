### Reproducing the bridge install error


### The cause of the bridge install error

`SharedTestnetBridge.install_on` passes `main_win` to `QObject.__init__` as the Qt
parent, so the stand-in window must itself be a `QObject`. The driver's stand-in
subclassed `object`.

### The correction to the bridge install

The stand-in subclasses `QObject`. The defect was in the driver, not in the product;
no product line changed.

### The renamed bucket's total broken, and caught

`Quintessence.respawn` credits the wallet in full. Its pleroma debit was halved, so the
four buckets hold more than was ever minted.

```
pleromaTotal -= amount;      ->  pleromaTotal -= amount / 2;

FAIL   invariant_fourBucketsEqualTotalEverMinted   buckets do not equal totalEverMinted
PASS   the other 15
```

One invariant, precisely the bucket law, and nothing else. A second break halving the
credit in `releaseAllToPleroma` was caught earlier still, by the suite's own `setUp`
requirement that five ore-scale movements leave 0.000000033 in the pleroma. Both files
were restored from the index.
