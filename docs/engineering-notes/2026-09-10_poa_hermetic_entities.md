# PoA — where entities and conflict sit in the hermetic framework

Reference. The operator asked two questions on 2026-09-10. This page answers both
from sources, under his sourcing standard of the same day.

> "Just need to find where and how ancient demons or entities map to or manifest
> within the hermetic framework and move forward from there. Not sure what sort of
> supernatural warfare ties into hermeticism as I have mostly read about it being a
> process of self transformation while using a particular language and set of
> symbols that serves a magical and meditative verse."

> "Does hermeticism have its own demonology or tie into demonology of its era."

## How to read this page

```

## The short answer

**HIS.** His reading of hermeticism is right. The philosophical texts are about
self-transformation, and they describe no warfare.

**SOURCED.** They still carry entities, and those entities run the cosmos rather
than fight in it. The founding treatise sets seven Rulers over the sensible world
and calls their rule Fate.

```
Corpus Hermeticum I.9

  "...formed Seven Rulers who enclose the cosmos that the sense perceives.
   Men call their ruling Fate."

  G.R.S. Mead, Thrice-Greatest Hermes, 1906
```

**MINE.** Hermeticism has entities from its first page and conflict on none of them.
It gives a chain of command, a penalty for the soul that fails, and a table of
afflictions. It gives no battle and no bestiary of enemies to kill.

## 1. The ascent through seven zones — the lead holds, with one correction

**SOURCED.** Treatise I describes the soul rising after death and giving up one
thing at each of seven zones. The list is explicit and ordered.

```
Corpus Hermeticum I.25, Mead 1906

  1  the Energy of Growth and Waning
  2  Device of Evils, now de-energized
  3  the Guile of the Desires, de-energized
  4  his Domineering Arrogance, de-energized
  5  unholy Daring and the Rashness of Audacity, de-energized
  6  Striving for Wealth by evil means
  7  Ensnaring Falsehood, de-energized
```

**SOURCED.** After the seventh the soul reaches the Eighth and sings with those
that are there.

```
Corpus Hermeticum I.26, Mead 1906

  "And then, with all the energisings of the harmony stript from him, clothed
   in his proper Power, he cometh to that Nature which belongs unto the Eighth,
   and there with those-that-are hymneth the Father."
```

**The correction.** The lead called these zones gatekeepers. The text names no
guardian at any zone. The soul returns an energy to the zone that gave it; nothing
challenges it, demands a toll, or can refuse it. The passage is a shedding, not a
series of encounters.

**MINE.** The level-by-level shape is still real and usable. Seven stations, each
one taking back a named fault, from the tradition's own founding document.

## 2. The thirty-six decans — the lead holds, with one correction

**SOURCED.** A Greek hermetic text of the Roman period gives all thirty-six decans
a secret name, an image to engrave, a stone, a plant, a food to avoid, and the part
of the body each one governs.

```
The Sacred Book of Hermes to Asclepius

  date        Roman-period Greek, surviving in later manuscripts
  edition     C.-E. Ruelle, Revue de philologie 32 (1908);
              Catalogus Codicum Astrologorum Graecorum
  study       "Decanal Iconography and Natural Materials in the Sacred Book of
              Hermes to Asclepius", Greek Roman and Byzantine Studies

  each entry  a name, an image, a stone, a plant, a food prohibition,
              and the body part the decan rules
```

**SOURCED.** The book's own preface states its purpose, and the purpose is
protective.

```
The Sacred Book of Hermes to Asclepius, preface

  "if you carry them on your person you will have a great phylactery, for all
   the afflictions that plague mankind through the influence of the stars may
   be healed thereby."
```

**The correction.** The lead called this a roster of named powers that cause harm.
The text is narrower and more exact. Each decan owns a body part and the afflictions
of that part, and the amulet invokes the same decan to heal them. A decan is the
authority over an affliction, on both sides of it. A decan does not attack.

**SOURCED.** A second hermetic text places the decans in the cosmos and gives them
servants. Its subject is the general disaster rather than the private illness.

```
Stobaean Hermetica 6, Hermes to Tat, On the Decans

  position   a circle beyond the zodiac, above the seven planetary spheres
  office     guardians and overseers of the universe; they move the planets
  below them daimones as their offspring, and leitourgoi as their assistants
  effect     overthrows of kings, uprisings, famines, plagues, earthquakes,
             and none of these without the energies of the decans

  Walter Scott, Hermetica III; M. D. Litwa, Hermetica II
```

**MINE.** This is the authentically ancient roster the design was looking for. It
is thirty-six named powers with images already described, a rank of servants beneath
them, and a fixed domain each.

## 3. The torment of matter — the lead holds for Zosimos, and fails as a doctrine

**SOURCED.** The visions of Zosimos of Panopolis describe a priest on a bowl-shaped
altar of fifteen steps. He announces an unbearable torment. A man then dismembers
him, parts his flesh from his bones, burns his head, and he becomes spirit.

```
Zosimos of Panopolis, the Visions, first vision

  the priest   Ion, "priest of the inner sanctuaries"
  the act      dismembered "in accordance with the rule of harmony",
               flesh separated from bones, the head burned
  the outcome  "burned (them) upon the fire of the art, till I perceived by the
               transformation of the body that I had become spirit"

  edition      The Visions of Zosimos of Panopolis: Text, Translation and
               Commentary, Oxford University Research Archive
```

**SOURCED.** The same corpus figures the base metals as people the work sacrifices
before they rise as noble metals, and the apparatus as temples and altars.

**The correction.** "The torture of matter" as a general law of alchemy is not an
ancient doctrine. C. G. Jung's twentieth-century reading supplies it, and his
standard excludes that reading. The ancient text supplies its own word: the priest
announces a torment, and the operation is a death followed by a rising.

**MINE.** The tradition already supplies enough. The dark register belongs to it
rather than arriving from outside.

## 4. Operative practice with entities — the lead holds, and the boundary is porous

**SOURCED.** The Greek Magical Papyri are the surviving handbooks of Greco-Roman
Egypt. Binding, compulsion, the expulsion of a daimon and the wearing of a
phylactery are all in them.

```
Greek Magical Papyri, Betz translation, 1986

  PGM IV.3007-3086   "a tested charm of Pibechis for those possessed by daimons"
                     an adjuration that drives out the daimon, then a phylactery
                     inscribed with divine names, worn by the patient
  PGM VIII.1-63      attributed to Astrapsoukos; invokes Hermes by name —
                     "Come to me, lord Hermes, as fetuses do to the wombs of women"
```

**SOURCED.** The two bodies are not sealed off from each other. One hermetic prayer
survives in three witnesses at once, and one of the three is a magical papyrus.

```
The Prayer of Thanksgiving, three witnesses

  Latin    Asclepius 41, inside the philosophical dialogue
  Coptic   Nag Hammadi Codex VI,7
  Greek    PGM III.591-611, inside a magical handbook
```

**SOURCED.** The scholarly convention sorts the material into philosophical and
technical hermetica. A.-J. Festugière drew the line hard. Garth Fowden and later
work softened it, and recent studies question it. The division is a tool for
sorting, not a fact about two separate religions.

**MINE.** The honest statement for the design is the middle one. The magical papyri
are not the Corpus Hermeticum, they share its vocabulary and its Hermes, and in at
least one case they share a text with it word for word.

## 5. Daimonology, not demonology — the lead holds, with one correction

**SOURCED.** In Greek a daimōn is an intermediary. The standing definition of the
period puts them between gods and men and gives them an office.

```
Apuleius, De deo Socratis

  position   between men and gods, "by place and region" and in nature of mind
  office     ministers of the gods, custodians and interpreters of men
  after      Plato, Symposium 202e
```

**SOURCED.** The hermetica place daimones inside that frame and give them a rank.
Three texts do it, and they agree on the shape while differing on the level.

```
where each text ranks the daimones

  Corpus Hermeticum XVI.13-16   under the stars; ministers of each star;
                                their essence is activity; authority over
                                earthly things; they act on the body — nerves,
                                veins, arteries, brain, heart
  Stobaean Hermetica 6          under the decans, as their offspring, with
                                the leitourgoi as assistants
  Asclepius 37-38               drawn down into statues by human craft
```

**The correction.** The lead placed the daimones under the decans in Corpus
Hermeticum XVI. They are under the stars there. The ranking beneath the decans is
Stobaean Hermetica 6. Two different texts, two different levels, and the design
should cite each for what it says.

**SOURCED.** The same treatise sets a limit on what a daimon can touch, and the
limit is the rational soul.

```
Corpus Hermeticum XVI.16, Mead 1906

  "The soul's rational part is set above the lordship of the daimons — designed
   to be receptacle of God... upon them the daimons do not act; for no one of
   the daimons or of Gods has any power against one Ray of God."
```

**SOURCED.** The punishing daimon is real, and Treatise I carries it. Its work is
retribution, and it makes the soul worse so that the penalty grows.

```
Corpus Hermeticum I.23, Mead 1906

  "...he yields his place to the Avenging Daimon, who sharpening the fire,
   tormenteth him and addeth fire to fire upon him, and rusheth upon him
   through his senses, thus rendering him readier for transgressions of the
   law, so that he meets with greater torment."
```

**SOURCED.** Treatise XIII names the afflictions of matter directly. The Greek word
for them shares its root with the avenging daimon above: timōria, retribution.

```
Corpus Hermeticum XIII.7-10

  twelve tormentors  ignorance, grief, incontinence, desire, injustice, greed,
                     deceit, envy, treachery, anger, recklessness, malice
  driven out by      ten powers; the birth in understanding completes when
                     the Ten drives out the Twelve
```

**SOURCED.** The statue passage is the clearest entity-handling in the
philosophical corpus, and its early reception proves what it claims.

```
Asclepius 37-38

  "Because they could not make souls, they mixed this power in and called up
   the souls of demons or angels and implanted them in likenesses through holy
   and divine mysteries, whence the idols could have the power to do good and
   evil."

Augustine, City of God VIII.23, quoting Hermes against him

  "man is the maker of the gods who are in the temples"
```

**SOURCED.** Hermeticism shares its milieu. Middle Platonic daimonology, the magical
papyri, Egyptian temple religion and Jewish material all run in the same centuries
and in the same Egypt. Hermeticism sits inside that world rather than beside it.

**MINE.** The answer to his second question: hermeticism has a daimonology, not a
demonology, and it invented neither. It took its era's and gave it a rank order.

## Executors of necessity — supported in substance, and the phrase is mine

**MINE.** The phrase "executors of necessity" is mine. The texts do not use
"necessity"; their word is Fate.

**SOURCED.** Four texts support what the phrase describes, and each one points the
same way.

```
what the sources actually say about hostility

  Corpus Hermeticum I.9     the Seven Rulers' rule IS Fate — administration
  Stobaean Hermetica 6      the decans are overseers; disasters follow their
                            energies, with no motive stated
  Corpus Hermeticum XVI     daimones are ministers; their essence is activity
  Corpus Hermeticum I.23    the avenging daimon delivers retribution
```

**SOURCED.** Nothing in the hermetica supplies a rebellion, a fall, a devil, or sin
as the cause of hostility. The afflictions are matter and ignorance, and the remedy
is knowledge.

**MINE.** Hermetic antagonists are cold rather than wicked, and that does converge
with the Lovecraftian register he has already set. An indifferent executor and an
indifferent cosmos are the same temperature.

## The enemy roster does not take one framing, and that is the finding

**SOURCED.** His roster of ancient entities splits four ways against its own texts.
Only the first group matches the executor framing.

```
executors of a mandate or of fate

  the gallû     Ereshkigal's enforcers; they take Dumuzi to satisfy the rule
                that nobody leaves the underworld unmarked
                Descent of Inanna to the Underworld
  Ammit         devours the heart that fails the weighing against Ma'at
                Book of the Dead, spell 125
  the Keres     daughters of Nyx, agents of the Moirai and of Doom; they seize
                the dying                             Hesiod, Theogony
  the Sebitti   created by Anu; they follow Erra into battle as his weapons
                Erra and Ishum
  Humbaba       set as guardian of the Cedar Forest by Enlil, as a terror to
                human beings                          Epic of Gilgamesh
  Mot           death itself, in the Ugaritic Baal cycle

afflicters with an appetite and no mandate

  Lamashtu      attacks mothers and infants; Mesopotamian incantations
  Lilith        from the lilitu onward
  Empousa       Greek
  Asmodeus      kills bridegrooms; driven off by Raphael   Tobit

  Apophis       attacks the solar barque nightly
  Anzû          steals the Tablet of Destinies
  Asag          disease demon; slain by Ninurta          Lugal-e

                                  forbidden knowledge, are bound in the abyss

**MINE.** The design must not flatten these into one register. The executor framing
fits the first group exactly, fits the second and third only partly, and contradicts
the fourth — 1 Enoch and the Zoroastrian material carry exactly the rebellion and
the moral evil that hermeticism lacks.

**MINE.** The split is useful rather than awkward. Four groups is four enemy classes
with four different behaviours, each one sourced, and each one legible to a player
without a lecture.

## The qliphoth and hermetic hostility do agree, with one caveat

**SOURCED.** The kelipot are the husks produced from the shards of the vessels that
broke at creation. Sparks of light fell with the shards, and the husks hold them.
The husks constitute the forces of the other side, the sitra ahra.

```
Lurianic Kabbalah

  tzimtzum           the contraction
  shevirat ha-kelim  the breaking of the vessels
  the kelipot        husks or shells from the shards, holding trapped sparks
  tiqqun             the restoration

  recorded by Hayyim Vital, Sefer Etz Hayyim, read back into the Zohar
```

**MINE.** The two sides do agree on shape. Hermetic hostility is functional, and the
kelipot are residue rather than sinners. Neither side needs a fall to explain why a
thing is hostile.

**The caveat.** The sources do call the kelipot evil forces. What is absent is the
narrative of rebellion and the figure of the sinner, not the category of evil.
Saying the kelipot are "not moral" overstates it. Saying they are impurity rather
than transgression is exact.

## What a design must not claim

**MINE.** Each line below is a claim the sources do not support. Stating them would
make the design wrong and would be easy to catch.

```
do not claim

  that hermeticism is a combat system
      the remedy in every philosophical treatise is knowledge, not a weapon

  that the decans are demons
      they are star powers, and the text invokes them to heal

  that daimones are devils
      no fall, no rebellion, no sin; they are ministers and executors

  that the Greek Magical Papyri are the Hermetica
      overlapping corpora that share a Hermes and at least one prayer

  that the qliphothic descent describes anyone's practice
      it is a structure borrowed from a living tradition

  that "the torture of matter" is ancient doctrine
      the phrase is Jung's; the torment in Zosimos is the text's own

  that the Old One Avatars are ancient
      Lovecraft is modern fiction, and the design should say so plainly

  that a reconstruction is an attestation
      the decan names and images are in the text; a Victorian Egypt is not
```

## The honesty note this page carries

**MINE.** The design adopts two traditions rather than inventing them, and both are
alive. Kabbalah is a living religious tradition, and Egyptian and Mesopotamian
material belongs to real historical religions.

**MINE.** The design borrows a structure. A floor is a sphere and its inversion is a
husk, because the shape is useful. That is the whole claim. It says nothing about
what anyone believes, and the design should never present it as more.

**MINE.** Where only the excluded revival layer supplies a word, the design names
the gap instead of reaching for the word. That rule is already in the issue and this
page inherits it.

## What I could not source

```
the full thirty-six decan list, verbatim
    the structure is confirmed and the roster itself is behind the editions:
    Classical Philology 117 (2022), and Catalogus Codicum Astrologorum Graecorum.
    Naming the thirty-six needs one of those in hand.

Copenhaver's translation, quoted
    in copyright. Every quotation on this page is Mead 1906, which is not.

the twelve tormentors from a primary text, in order
    the order above comes from a report of Copenhaver's translation rather than
    from the Greek. The manuscript tradition of this list is uneven — the seven
    planetary pairs are better attested than the twelve.

Stobaean Hermetica 6, section numbers
    the content is confirmed through Scott and Litwa. The internal numbering
    could not be read.
```

## What turned out wrong

```
Corpus Hermeticum XVI does not rank daimones under the decans
    it ranks them under the stars. Stobaean Hermetica 6 is the decan text.

the seven zones have no gatekeepers
    the soul returns an energy to each zone. Nothing guards, demands or refuses.

"the torture of matter" is not an attested doctrine
    Zosimos's own torment is. The general law is a modern reading.

PGM VIII.1-63 is a favour request, not a binding
    the papyrus titles it a binding love spell and its content asks Hermes for
    favour. Cite it for the Hermes invocation. For binding and expulsion, cite
    PGM IV.3007-3086.
```
