## WHAT ACERVATOR IS

A desktop **accumulation** platform trading real money on Coinbase across
**38 live bots**. It accumulates base assets by scrumming a slice of profit off
intra-cycle volatility and folding it back into base position size. Every
completed Scrum/Fold cycle ends holding more of the asset.

**Not** a grid bot, DCA bot, or rebalancer.

present during the conversion. Never cite Qt as the architecture when writing
